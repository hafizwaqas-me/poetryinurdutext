## Allama Iqbal Poetry Collection

علامہ اقبال کی شاعری اردو ادب کا قیمتی سرمایہ ہے۔ ان کی شاعری میں خودی، ملت، اور عشق رسول ﷺ کے موضوعات نمایاں ہیں۔

Allama Muhammad Iqbal (1877-1938) was a poet, philosopher, and politician who is widely regarded as having inspired the Pakistan Movement. His poetry focuses on self-improvement (Khudi), Islamic unity, and spiritual awakening.
