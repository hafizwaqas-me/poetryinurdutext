# Mirza Ghalib Poetry - مرزا غالب کی شاعری

Mirza Asadullah Khan Ghalib (1797-1869) was one of the most influential poets of the Urdu and Persian languages. His ghazals are celebrated for their depth, wit, and philosophical insight.

## About Ghalib

Ghalib lived during the last years of the Mughal Empire and witnessed its decline. His poetry reflects the turmoil of his times while exploring timeless themes of love, life, and death.

## Famous Works

- **Diwan-e-Ghalib**: His complete collection of Urdu ghazals
- **Kulliyat-e-Ghalib**: Persian poetry collection
- **Letters**: His letters are considered masterpieces of Urdu prose

## Themes in Ghalib's Poetry

- **Ishq (Love)**: Both divine and earthly love
- **Philosophy**: Existential and metaphysical questions
- **Wine and Beloved**: Classical ghazal imagery
- **Life's Struggles**: Personal hardships and resilience
