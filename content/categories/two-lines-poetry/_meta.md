# Two Lines Poetry - دو لائن شاعری

Two line poetry (دو لائن شاعری) is perfect for expressing emotions concisely. These short verses pack powerful meanings in just two lines, making them ideal for social media status updates and sharing with friends.

## Why Two Line Poetry is Popular

- Easy to remember and share
- Perfect for WhatsApp and Instagram status
- Expresses deep emotions in few words
- Great for quick reading
