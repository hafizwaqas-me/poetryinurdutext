# Urdu Poetry - اردو شاعری

Explore our extensive collection of classic and contemporary Urdu poetry. From ghazals to nazms, from classical masters to modern poets, we bring you the best of Urdu literature.

## Forms of Urdu Poetry

- **Ghazal (غزل)**: The most popular form of Urdu poetry
- **Nazm (نظم)**: Poetry with a consistent theme
- **Rubai (رباعی)**: Four-line poems
- **Qawwali (قوالی)**: Sufi devotional poetry
