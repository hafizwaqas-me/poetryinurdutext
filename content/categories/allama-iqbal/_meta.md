# Allama Iqbal Poetry - علامہ اقبال کی شاعری

Allama Muhammad Iqbal (1877-1938), known as the "Poet of the East" (شاعر مشرق), was one of the most influential Urdu and Persian poets. His poetry focused on spiritual revival and political freedom of the Muslim community.

## Famous Works

- **Shikwa and Jawab-e-Shikwa**: A dialogue with God
- **Bang-e-Dra**: Collection of early poetry
- **Bal-e-Jibril**: One of his finest works
- **Zarb-e-Kalim**: Later philosophical poetry

## Themes in Iqbal's Poetry

- **Khudi (Self)**: The concept of self-realization
- **Mard-e-Momin**: The ideal Muslim
- **Philosophy of Action**: Against passive acceptance
- **Unity of Muslim Ummah**: Pan-Islamic vision
