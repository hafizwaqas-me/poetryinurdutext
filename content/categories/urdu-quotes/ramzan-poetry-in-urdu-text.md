## Ramzan Poetry in Urdu Text

Ramzan is the month of fasting, patience, self-control, forgiveness and
Quran. In Urdu poetry, fasting is often described as a way for the heart to
learn patience, and sehri and iftari mark the rhythm of each day. This
collection covers the arrival of Ramzan, the value of roza, the joy of
iftari, and the sadness of Alvida Ramzan, so readers can share a verse that
matches the day they are in.
