## Islamic Poetry in Urdu 2 Lines

Islamic poetry in two lines is used for daily reminders: iman, sabr,
shukr, dua, namaz and Quran. These short verses are easy to read, easy to
remember and easy to share, and they work well as status lines and morning
messages. This collection keeps the message clear and respectful, with no
claim that these are classical poet verses.
