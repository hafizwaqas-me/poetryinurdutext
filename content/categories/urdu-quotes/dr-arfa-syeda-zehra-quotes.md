### Dr. Arfa Syeda Zehra Famous Quotes List

Dr. Arfa Syeda Zehra is a beacon of knowledge. Her **famous sayings in Urdu** often highlight the importance of "Khudi" (Self-respect) and moral values.

> **"Taleem ka maqsad sirf degree hasil karna nahi balkay zindagi ko behtar banana hai."**
> (The purpose of education is not just to get a degree but to make life better.)

This quote resonates deeply with students and educators alike, emphasizing the true essence of learning.

### Dr. Arfa Syeda Zehra Quotes on Women (Aurat Quotes)

Dr. Arfa has always been a strong advocate for women's rights and their role in society. Her **quotes on women** inspire confidence and strength.

**"Aurat ke paas koi kamzor taqat nahi, woh apne dil ki taqat se sab kuch jeet sakti hai."**
(A woman possesses no weak power; she can win everything with the strength of her heart.)

These words encourage women to believe in themselves and their capabilities.
