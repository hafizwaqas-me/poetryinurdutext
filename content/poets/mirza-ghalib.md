# Mirza Ghalib (1797-1869)

Mirza Asadullah Baig Khan, known by his pen name Ghalib, was born on December 27, 1797, in Agra, India. He is considered one of the greatest poets of the Urdu language and a significant figure in Persian poetry.

## Early Life

Ghalib was born into a family of Turkish aristocrats who had immigrated to India. He was orphaned at a young age and raised by his uncle. He began writing poetry at the age of eleven and was already a recognized poet by his teens.

## Life in Delhi

Ghalib spent most of his adult life in Delhi, then the capital of the Mughal Empire. He witnessed the decline of the empire and the Indian Rebellion of 1857, which profoundly affected him and his poetry.

## Literary Style

Ghalib's poetry is known for its:

- **Philosophical depth**: Exploring questions of existence and meaning
- **Wit and wordplay**: Creative use of language and metaphors
- **Romantic imagery**: Beautiful expressions of love and longing
- **Persian influence**: Sophisticated style drawn from Persian traditions

## Major Works

- **Diwan-e-Ghalib**: His complete collection of Urdu ghazals
- **Kulliyat-e-Ghalib**: Persian poetry collection
- **Dastanbuy**: A memoir of the 1857 events in Delhi
- **Letters (Khatoot-e-Ghalib)**: Considered masterpieces of Urdu prose

## Famous Lines

His verses like "Hazaron khwahishein aisi" and "Dil-e-nadaan tujhe hua kya hai" are widely quoted and loved. His poetry continues to be sung by prominent artists and is studied in literature courses worldwide.

## Legacy

Ghalib died on February 15, 1869, in Delhi. His tomb in Nizamuddin, Delhi, is visited by admirers from around the world. He is celebrated as one of the greatest poets in Urdu literature.
