# Top 10 Famous Ghazal Poets of All Time

The ghazal is the most beloved form of Urdu poetry, and these ten poets have mastered its art like no other. Their verses continue to touch hearts and inspire new generations of poetry lovers.

## 1. Mirza Ghalib (1797-1869)

Often considered the greatest Urdu poet, Ghalib's ghazals are celebrated for their philosophical depth and linguistic brilliance. His verse "Hazaron khwahishein aisi" remains one of the most quoted lines in Urdu poetry.

## 2. Mir Taqi Mir (1723-1810)

Known as the "Khuda-e-Sukhan" (God of Poetry), Mir's ghazals express profound emotions with remarkable simplicity. His poetry about love and loss continues to resonate with readers.

## 3. Allama Muhammad Iqbal (1877-1938)

The poet-philosopher who gave Urdu poetry a new dimension with his concept of Khudi (self). His ghazals inspire self-improvement and spiritual awakening.

## 4. Faiz Ahmed Faiz (1911-1984)

The revolutionary poet who combined romantic imagery with social consciousness. His ghazals speak of love while addressing broader human struggles.

## 5. Ahmad Faraz (1931-2008)

Known for his accessible yet profound poetry, Faraz became one of the most popular modern ghazal poets. His work is widely sung by musicians.

## 6. Jaun Elia (1931-2002)

The enigmatic poet of alienation whose pessimistic yet beautiful ghazals captured the modern condition. His poetry has gained immense popularity posthumously.

## 7. Momin Khan Momin (1800-1852)

A contemporary of Ghalib, Momin was known for his romantic ghazals. Even Ghalib praised his famous verse about sacrificing his entire poetry.

## 8. Meer Dard (1721-1785)

The Sufi poet whose ghazals seamlessly blend divine and earthly love. His poetry reflects deep spiritual consciousness.

## 9. Sahir Ludhianvi (1921-1980)

Known for his film lyrics and revolutionary poetry, Sahir brought ghazal to mainstream audiences through cinema.

## 10. Parveen Shakir (1952-1994)

One of the most celebrated female poets, Parveen Shakir brought a fresh feminine perspective to the ghazal tradition.

## Conclusion

These poets have left an indelible mark on Urdu literature. Their ghazals continue to be recited, sung, and cherished by millions around the world.
